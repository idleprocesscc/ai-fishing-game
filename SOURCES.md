# Research sources and content policy

World Waters Field Journal uses English canonical records and concise Chinese
player-facing summaries. Facts are paraphrased rather than copied. Scientific
names, distribution, identification, life history, and conservation claims
should be checked against government agencies, research institutions, river
commissions, or primary scientific literature before new content is merged.

The game's conservation flags are educational abstractions. Fishing laws,
seasons, possession limits, and protected status change over time; the game
must never present itself as current legal advice.

## Cape waters

Cape-region records currently use conservative, broadly established habitat
and identification summaries. Before the region grows, add South African
government or primary fisheries references for every new species and for any
claim about current retention rules.
